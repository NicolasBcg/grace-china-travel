The challenge

China is amazing.
Organising it from Europe is not.

More and more of your clients want to visit China. But a lot of things work differently there — and when something goes wrong, it is hard to fix from 9,000 km away.

💳

Payments

China runs on Alipay and WeChat Pay. Setting them up with a foreign card is not always easy, and cash is rarely used.

📱

Apps & internet

Google, WhatsApp and Maps don't work without a VPN. Taxis, food and tickets go through Chinese apps.

🎫

Real-name bookings

Trains and major sites need the traveller's passport, and popular slots sell out days in advance.

🗣️

Language

Most drivers, small hotels and restaurants speak little or no English.

🆘

Problems on the ground

A missing driver, a cancelled train, a hotel issue: someone needs to call in Chinese, right now.

🧭

Generic tours

Big ground operators sell standard circuits. Your clients want a trip that fits them.

About me

Hi, I'm Grace

"I'd love to visit China, but it looks so complicated." I heard this everywhere I lived.

I'm Chinese, and I've spent the last years living abroad — first in Barcelona, then in Cambodia. Around me, people kept saying the same thing: the language, the apps, the payments, the train tickets, the sites you need to book days in advance with your passport… China felt out of reach.

So I started planning their trips. I designed their itineraries, booked what needed to be booked in Chinese, and stayed reachable while they travelled. They came back amazed — and recommended me to their friends, who asked me to do the same, this time as a paid service.

Today I'm back in China, in Shenzhen, and I work with travel agencies across Europe who want to offer China to their clients without the headaches. Your clients stay your clients. I just make China work for them.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask

Do I keep the relationship with my clients?

Yes. You sell the trip under your brand and keep your margin. I can talk to your travellers in your agency's name, or as your China specialist — as you prefer.

Who is responsible for the trip?

Your agency remains the organiser of the trip. I provide a service to your agency: trip design, local know-how and support.

When can travellers reach you?

Every day from 8 am to 10 pm China time, emergencies included. At night, travellers use the local emergency numbers and their travel insurance assistance, and I take over again in the morning.

How much does it cost?

You pay per trip, with no subscription. The price depends on the length of the trip and the options you choose. I offer special conditions for your first trips — let's talk about it on a call.

Do you work with my existing local partner in China?

Yes. I can design the trip and support your travellers while your usual ground operator handles the bookings — or I can help you find trusted local suppliers.

What if my agency doesn't sell China yet?

That's a great place to start. I can help you build your first China trips and answer your team's questions.
